10. LIMITATIONS

---

The BERT model used in this project was fine-tuned using a
small synthetic dataset containing only 60 training examples.

Therefore, the benchmark results should not be interpreted as
representative of real-world BERT performance.

The benchmark dataset is also a shortened ISEAR-derived dataset
with four emotions rather than the six emotions used by the
project's final emotion classification system.

The missing Surprise and Disgust categories were not treated as
errors. The benchmark was evaluated only using the four emotion
categories available in the dataset.
